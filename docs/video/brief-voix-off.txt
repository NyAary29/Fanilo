TEXTE À LIRE (anglais)

Scene 01 — 0:00 to 0:07 — 7 seconds max
In Madagascar, many women and girls have no easy way to get reliable health
information in their own language. FANILO changes that.

Scene 02 — 0:07 to 0:28 — 21 seconds max
Hanta is sixteen. She has a question she doesn't dare to ask out loud.
With FANILO, she needs no account and no name. She asks in Malagasy, by voice
or in writing, and gets an answer drawn only from messages approved by
health professionals.

Scene 03 — 0:28 to 0:43 — 15 seconds max
When the question needs a human, it goes to a counselor, who classifies it
and writes what Hanta should do.

Scene 04 — 0:43 to 0:57 — 14 seconds max
The reply comes back to Hanta's phone. Still anonymous. Still private.

Scene 05 — 0:57 to 1:18 — 21 seconds max
In the village, Lova, a community health worker, registers Voahirana.
Consent comes first, and the app works even without network.

Scene 06 — 1:18 to 1:38 — 20 seconds max
Her pregnancy is recorded. Her antenatal visits are scheduled, and reminders
are planned automatically.

Scene 07 — 1:38 to 1:57 — 19 seconds max
During a visit, Lova notices a danger sign. One tap, and the health centre is alerted.

Scene 08 — 1:57 to 2:12 — 15 seconds max
The alert goes to a named person, with a response deadline and a backup.
Nothing is promised until a health professional confirms.

Scene 09 — 2:12 to 2:30 — 18 seconds max
Health centres follow pregnancies, missed visits, alerts and referrals,
with anonymised data only.

Scene 10 — 2:30 to 2:36 — 6 seconds max
FANILO never diagnoses. AI proposes, health professionals decide.
