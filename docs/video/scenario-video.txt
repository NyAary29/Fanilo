PARTIE 2 — FACE CAMÉRA (filmée par l'équipe)
--------------------------------------------
Conseils : cadrage épaule, lumière de face, 1 ou 2 personnes, phrases courtes.
Afficher le nom + la fonction en bas de l'écran (je l'ajoute au montage).
Durée ~1 min 15. Texte proposé en anglais (à dire avec vos mots, pas lu mot à mot).

1. LE PROBLÈME (~25 s)
   "In Madagascar, too many women start antenatal care late, and many miss their
    postnatal follow-up. Reliable health information is rarely available in spoken
    Malagasy, many women read with difficulty, and in rural areas a phone is often
    shared. When a danger sign appears, precious time is lost before reaching care."
   [Si vous citez un chiffre, il doit être sourcé : le cahier des charges l'exige.]

2. NOTRE SOLUTION (~25 s)
   "FANILO is a voice assistant in Malagasy. Women and girls ask their questions by
    voice, anonymously, and get answers validated by health professionals. Community
    health workers follow pregnancies offline, and a danger sign sends an alert to
    the health centre. AI helps, but it never decides alone."

3. L'AVENIR ET L'IMPACT (~25 s)
   "We are preparing a pilot in Bongolava and Antananarivo, with three health centres,
    twenty-five community health workers and about 250 to 300 women. Our goals:
    more women starting antenatal care early, referrals that really reach the health
    centre, and open-source voice tools for the Malagasy language that others can reuse.
    With Havanana Impact and Creatic — health for everyone, by voice."

   (Objectifs = cibles du pilote, à présenter comme des objectifs, pas des résultats.)

CARTON DE FIN (5 s, ajouté au montage)
  « FANILO — Havanana Impact × Creatic »
  Sous-titre : AI proposes. Health professionals decide.
